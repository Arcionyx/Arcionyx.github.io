Assumptions & calculation rules

One buy and one sell in this demo. Capacity: 10,000 units; daily injection/withdrawal caps: 5,000 each; injection and withdrawal fees: USD 0.02/unit each; transport: 0; discount rate: 0. No transit delay. Storage is billed per started month anchored to the injection date; same-day storage costs zero. Inventory must close at zero. Same-day events inject first, then withdraw.
