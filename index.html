I’m interested in what happens between a model, a system, and the decision someone needs to make.

At Rutgers Business School, I study Business Analytics and Information Technology. My experience brings together e-commerce operations in Ningbo, a Disney strategy study, and quantitative research simulations covering energy prices and credit risk.

The common thread is translation: from messy information to a model, from a model to a workflow, and from analysis to a clear business recommendation. My interests in equity research, financial markets, and entrepreneurship give that work direction.
